Catalogue entry I

Choosing a Work Bag

A good leather work bag can last twenty years. Choosing well means matching its shape to your day, then checking the leather and the build.

Read time
7 minutes
Covers
Shape, leather, build
Updated
November 18, 2026
01

Start with your day

How you travel decides the shape. Train and car commuters often like a satchel or briefcase with a top handle. Cyclists and walkers usually prefer a messenger or backpack that leaves both hands free. If you carry a laptop every day, plan around that first.

02

Know your leathers

LeatherLook and feelAges
Full-grainNatural surface, visible grainDevelops a rich patina
Top-grainLightly sanded, more uniformWears evenly
Vegetable-tannedFirm, earthy smell, darkensChanges the most
Chrome-tannedSofter, more colour optionsChanges little
SuedeBrushed, velvety napNeeds more protection
03

Check the build

  • Stitching: small, even and tight, especially at handle and strap anchors.
  • Edges: smooth, sealed or folded, not fraying.
  • Hardware: solid metal buckles and rings that feel heavy for their size.
  • Lining: sturdy and neatly sewn; a lined bag holds its shape longer.
  • Base: feet or a reinforced bottom protect it when set down.
04

Size it honestly

Bigger bags get filled. Choose the smallest bag that fits your laptop plus what you carry on a normal day, not on your busiest one. See the laptop fit entry for measuring.

05

Plan for care

Leather lasts when it is cleaned, conditioned and stored properly. Our care calendar spreads the work across the year so it never becomes a chore.
