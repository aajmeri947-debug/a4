About Satchel

A field guide, not a shop.

Satchel is an independent website about leather satchels and work bags.

We write for people who carry a bag to work every day and want it to fit their routine, their laptop and their back. Our guides explain shapes, leathers and construction in plain language, and our tools help you choose and look after a bag.

We do not sell bags, run affiliate links or accept payment from makers. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
