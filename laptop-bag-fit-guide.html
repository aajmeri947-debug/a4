Laptop Bag Fit

The most common work bag mistake is a laptop that does not quite fit. Measure first, then think about padding, weight and how you carry.

Read time
6 minutes
Covers
Measuring, padding, comfort
Updated
November 18, 2026
01

Measure the laptop, not the screen

The advertised screen size is measured diagonally and tells you little about the case. Measure the closed laptop’s width, depth and thickness, then add about 2 cm to width and depth for a sleeve or padding.

13″ laptopapprox. 30 × 21 cm
14″ laptopapprox. 32 × 23 cm
15–16″ laptopapprox. 36 × 25 cm

Typical sizes only. Always measure your own model.

02

Padding and protection

Many leather satchels have no padded compartment. A separate padded sleeve solves this and lets you move the laptop between bags. Look for a bag whose base keeps the laptop off the ground when you set it down.

03

Weight and straps

A laptop, charger and documents can easily reach 3–4 kg. For daily carry, a wide, padded shoulder strap spreads the load; a cross-body strap is kinder still. Check that the strap anchors are stitched and riveted, not just glued.

04

Try it loaded

If you can, try a bag with your laptop inside. Check that the flap closes without straining, the zip runs freely, and the bag sits flat against your side rather than twisting.

05

Switch it up

Change shoulders through the day and empty the bag weekly. Leather keeps its shape best when it is not stretched by a permanently overfilled load.
